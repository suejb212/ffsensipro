About Us

Helping Free Fire players discover sensitivity settings that match their devices and play styles.

FF Sensi Pro is an independent online Free Fire sensitivity recommendation tool created for players who want a simple way to explore settings for their phones.

Our Mission

Make sensitivity setup easier to understand, faster to explore and more accessible for Free Fire players using different devices.

🎮 What Is FF Sensi Pro?

FF Sensi Pro is a website that provides personalized starting sensitivity recommendations for Free Fire players.

Instead of searching through random sensitivity settings, visitors can select their device details and preferred play style to receive a suggested setup.

Our goal is to help players experiment with settings and find what feels comfortable for their own gameplay.

🚀 How It Works

Using the FF Sensi Pro generator is simple:

  1. Select your phone brand and model.
  2. Choose your RAM and screen refresh rate.
  3. Select your preferred play style.
  4. Choose a sensitivity scale.
  5. Tap Generate Sensitivity.
  6. Follow the on-screen steps to unlock and view your recommendations.
  7. Test the settings in Free Fire and adjust them to your preference.

💡 Why Sensitivity Matters

Sensitivity settings influence how quickly your in-game camera and aiming controls respond to your movements.

Different devices, screen sizes and individual playing habits can affect which settings feel comfortable.

There is no single perfect sensitivity setup for every player. Our generator provides a starting point that you can fine-tune through practice.
