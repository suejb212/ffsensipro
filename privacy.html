Privacy Policy

Your gaming experience matters, and so does your privacy. Here's how information is handled when you use FF Sensi Pro.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Welcome to FF Sensi Pro, a Free Fire sensitivity recommendation website available at ffsensipro.com. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you visit our website.

Our approach

You can use our free sensitivity generator without creating an account. We ask for device and play-style details to prepare your recommended settings.

1. Information Used by the Sensi Generator

To create personalized Free Fire sensitivity recommendations, our generator asks for your phone brand, phone model, RAM, screen refresh rate, play style and preferred sensitivity scale.

These details are submitted to our generation service to calculate recommended settings. You do not need to enter your real name, home address or phone number to use the free generator.

2. Website Usage and Technical Data

When you visit FF Sensi Pro, our hosting provider and website services may process information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and interactions with website features.

This information may support website delivery, security, performance, usage measurement and improvements.

3. Cookies and Advertising

FF Sensi Pro uses advertising-related services, including Google AdSense code and Monetag technology. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to display, personalize and measure advertisements, depending on which services are active and your privacy choices.

Advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses and browsing activity according to their own privacy policies.

Learn more about Google's advertising data practices and visit our Cookie Policy.

4. Analytics and Website Improvements

We may use website measurement tools to understand how visitors interact with the sensitivity generator, which pages are useful and where improvements may be needed.

Analytics and advertising tools may collect technical or usage information depending on the services configured on our website.

5. Premium Sensi and Payments

FF Sensi Pro includes a Premium Sensi section that may offer additional sensitivity recommendations for a one-time payment when purchases are available.

Premium checkout may be handled by an external payment provider such as Lemon Squeezy. Payment providers may process billing and transaction details under their own privacy policies.

The free sensitivity generator does not ask you to enter credit card information.

6. External Links

Our website includes links to other services, such as TikTok and external payment providers. When you leave FF Sensi Pro, the privacy practices of those websites apply.

7. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Generate sensitivity recommendations for your device.
  • Keep the website working and secure.
  • Improve the generator and visitor experience.
  • Measure website usage and advertising performance.
  • Support Premium services when available.

8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect our website. Information may be processed or retained by hosting, advertising, analytics and payment providers according to their respective policies.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into the generator's input fields.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct or request deletion of certain personal information.

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or advertising technologies, appropriate consent choices should be provided.

10. Children's Privacy

FF Sensi Pro is a gaming utility and does not require users to submit personal identity information to generate free settings.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has submitted personal information through the website, please contact us.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, features or third-party services change. Any updates will be published here with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy-related request, please visit our Contact Us page.
